Reel Fun Fishing HUD

Beta Tester User Guide

⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING
Always attach the HUD first before adding your License.
Adding the license before the HUD can cause validation issues.

Quick Overview

The Reel Fun Fishing HUD is a modern fish anywhere system. You do not need to be near Linden water or a special server. Your beta tester package includes 1 HUD + Infinite License.

License Info: Licenses provide unlimited bait and the ability to fish anywhere in Second Life. All licenses are cumulative. New licenses add to your remaining time.

1. Getting Started – Activate Your HUD & License

1

Attach the HUD

Right-click the HUD in your inventory and select Wear (or drag it onto your avatar).

Wait for the confirmation message in local chat:

✅ Valid Hud/user

Move the HUD to your preferred screen position. Click the logo to expand or minimize it.

Note: When wearing a fishing rod and you minimize the HUD, it will automatically detach your rod.
2

Add Your License

IMPORTANT: You must attach the HUD before adding the license.

Rez or attach your License, then click it. The license time will be added to your HUD and verified.

Once the "LICENSE" indicator on the HUD turns green, you are ready to get your rod.

2. How to Fish

3

Open Settings & Choose a Rod

Click the SETTINGS button on the HUD. A blue popup menu will appear.

Click FISHING.

Choose your rod type:

  • Salt Water – Generally produces bigger fish
  • Fresh Water – Can also produce monster-sized fish

The chosen rod will be delivered to your inventory. Wear the rod.

4

Cast & Catch Fish

Once the rod is worn, the CAST and CLEAR & BAIT buttons on the HUD will turn green.

Click CAST to cast your line.

Watch local chat to see what you caught (or didn’t catch).

Important: You must have the fishing pole attached for the cast operation to work.

After every successful catch, click CLEAR & BAIT to reset. Then click CAST again.

3. Running Multi-User Contests

Once you’ve fished for a while and want some friendly competition:

5

Start a Contest (as Host)

  1. Click SETTINGS on your HUD.
  2. Click CONTESTS.
  3. Click Default Contest (it will be labeled something like “Contest1”).

The contest will start and be announced in the region.

6

Join a Contest (as Participant)

Other anglers on the same sim should do the following:

  1. Open their HUD → SETTINGS → CONTESTS.
  2. Click Join “Contest1” (or the active contest name).

May the best angler win by total weight!

Current Limitations:
• Contests must be started and ended manually by the host.
• You can view Participants, End the contest, and check Scores from the menu.
• Contests cannot run while traveling between sims.

4. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Common Issues & Solutions

  • HUD or rod not responding?
    Most issues are fixed by simply detaching and re-attaching the HUD and/or fishing rod. Also make sure you have the latest version of the HUD.
  • Sim crossing problems?
    This is a HUD, not a vehicle. Avoid crossing sims at high speed or at sharp angles while the HUD is active. Slow troll fishing across sim lines works fine.
  • My catches aren’t being counted?
    You most likely logged off or crashed while the HUD was active. Contact Joe or Short immediately for a replacement.
  • Why do I have to click CLEAR & BAIT after every catch?
    This is by design. Many users like taking pictures with their catches. Auto-clear would remove that opportunity.
  • Can I run contests while moving between sims?
    No. All contests must stay on the sim where they were started.
Server Maintenance: Occasionally the external data servers go down for short maintenance. Keep fishing anyway — your catches will still accumulate even if stats temporarily stop updating.

5. Additional Notes

  • The fishing pole must be attached for the CAST button to function.
  • You must click CLEAR & BAIT after every catch before you can cast again.
  • Minimizing the HUD while wearing a rod will automatically detach the rod.
  • Currently supports up to 50 fish variations (more coming soon).
  • You can view your catches via the Tacklebox button (when available).
